Expira Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

Expira does not require an account. Item names, opening dates, expiry dates, icons, and packaging photos are stored on your device and are used to display items, calculate days remaining, show widgets, and schedule reminders. Reminder settings are also stored on your device.

You choose whether to take a photo or select one from your photo library. Expira has no developer-operated server and does not upload this information or share it with third parties. The system delivers reminders locally on your device.

Item records remain until you delete them. Swipe left on a record to delete it and its saved photo copy, or edit the item and choose Remove to delete the photo copy. These actions do not delete originals from your photo library. You can revoke camera or notification access at any time in iOS Settings.
